Systems, performance
& quality engineering

My background is in systems, performance, scale, and quality engineering, with hands-on work around Kubernetes, OpenShift, virtualization, and automation.

I’m interested in what happens between a promising design and a system people can depend on: the assumptions, the bottlenecks, the failure modes, and the evidence that tells us whether something actually works.

What I write about

This is my home for technical essays and engineering explorations. Some start with a practical problem. Others ask where software engineering might go next. When an idea can be tested, I want the models, experiments, and limitations to be part of the story.

Generation VII is the first publication here. It explores how autonomous agents might evolve software under independently enforced constraints, and where that proposal still needs evidence.

An open conversation

I welcome careful criticism, counterexamples, and alternative designs. Useful engineering ideas should survive scrutiny.
